I. THE CORRESPONDENCE DESK

All of it on one desk, in the order it arrived.

Filter by tag, version, device, OS or source. A short on-device summary sits on top, so you know what's working and what needs attention before you open a single note.

II. HOW A LETTER TRAVELS

From “it's broken” to “it's fixed”.

Everything is stored as issues and milestones in your own GitHub repository. No new service to run.

  1. NO. 1
    Received
    The SDK, an App Store review or an email lands as a labelled GitHub issue, with version, device and OS attached.
  2. NO. 2
    Read
    Apple Intelligence summarizes and translates on your device. A German note reads in English.
  3. NO. 3
    Filed
    Attach it to a task, give the task a priority, and slot it into a version.
  4. NO. 4
    Answered
    Reply from your own address. On release day, everyone whose note was fixed gets one personal email.
III. WRITING BACK

A reply is the kindest feature you can ship.

Send from your own mail account with your header and footer. Keep templates like “Thanks — we're on it” and “Fixed in the latest update” one tap away, from your Mac, iPad or iPhone.

“Good news: this is fixed in the update that just went out. Please update from the App Store.”

Send from anywhere. Arrives the same.

V. SEALED

Private letters stay private.

On-device AI
