Claude Pro vs Max vs Team: how much usage each plan gets

Anthropic doesn't publish message counts. It publishes multipliers, and they're all relative to Pro. Here's what each plan gets, what the numbers actually compare, and how to tell from your own usage whether an upgrade is worth it.

{{DATE_LONG}} · 2 min read

The plans side by side

US prices from claude.com/pricing and usage in Anthropic's own wording, as of October 18, 2026:

  • Free ($0): for everyday questions, with a session limit that resets every five hours.
  • Pro ($20/month, or $17/month billed yearly): at least 5x the usage of Free per 5-hour session.
  • Max 5x ($100/month): 5x Pro's usage per session.
  • Max 20x ($200/month): 20x Pro's usage per session.
  • Team Standard seat ($25/month, or $20/month billed yearly, at least 2 seats): 1.25x Pro per session.
  • Team Premium seat ($125/month, or $100/month billed yearly): 6.25x Pro per session.
  • Enterprise: the seat covers access, and all usage is billed at API rates, with no usage limits.

Sources: Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise in Anthropic's help center. Team limits apply to each member, not to the team as a whole.

The multipliers are per session, not per week. "20x Pro" describes the 5-hour session allowance. Anthropic doesn't publish a weekly multiplier, so don't assume Max 20x gives you twenty times Pro's weekly limit.

Models matter as much as the plan

  • Pro and Max have one weekly limit across all models.
  • Max adds a separate Fable weekly limit: Fable can use up to half of your weekly limit, from the same pool.
  • Pro and Team Standard don't include Fable models in the plan at all. They run only on usage credits (Fable models on your plan).

Within a plan, the model you pick changes how far your limit goes. In Claude Code, Anthropic notes that Opus "costs several times more per turn than Sonnet" (models, usage and limits in Claude Code).

Do you need to upgrade?

Look at which limit you actually hit:

  • You hit the session limit, but the weekly bar stays low. Your work comes in bursts. A bigger plan raises the session allowance, but so does spreading the work out, and usage credits can cover the odd heavy afternoon.
  • You hit the weekly limit days before it resets. That's the real sign you've outgrown your plan.
  • You rarely get past half of either. You may be able to step down a plan.

A week or two of history answers this better than a guess. Usage for Claude charts your session and weekly usage over time, predicts whether you'll hit a limit at your current pace, and shows each account's plan, so you can compare a Pro and a Max account side by side. When you do upgrade, the new plan's limits apply right away (Anthropic).
