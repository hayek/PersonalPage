ChatGPT Pro 100 vs 200 vs 500: how much Codex usage each tier gets

Pro comes in three tiers: $100, $200 and $500 a month. OpenAI doesn't publish exact allowances. It only says Pro 200 includes more Codex and Work usage than Pro 100, and Pro 500 the most. None of them has a 5-hour limit in Codex, and only Pro 500 gets Ultrafast.

What are the three ChatGPT Pro tiers?

ChatGPT Pro comes in three monthly plans (About ChatGPT Pro tiers):

  • Pro 100: $100/month. No Ultrafast.
  • Pro 200: $200/month. No Ultrafast.
  • Pro 500: $500/month. Includes Ultrafast for GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol.

All three are billed monthly only. Prices are in US dollars and can differ by country and app store.

How much Codex usage does each Pro tier get?

OpenAI doesn't publish exact numbers. Its wording is: "Pro 200 includes more usage than Pro 100. Pro 500 offers the highest included usage of the three plans." The allowance covers both Codex and ChatGPT Work, which share one pool.

You'll see multipliers like "50x Plus" quoted around the web. OpenAI's help center and pricing pages don't state them, so treat them as estimates. OpenAI also says the allowance "does not correspond to a fixed number of messages or tasks". How far it goes depends on the model, the task and your settings (Managing usage with GPT-6 Astra).

What changed with the Pro allowances recently?

  • Pro 100 and Pro 500 now include twice their previous Work and Codex allowance.
  • Pro 200: the reduction announced in OpenAI's September 29 email is canceled. If you had the lower allowance, it doubled to match the higher one. Pro 200 is open to new subscribers again, at the same $200.
  • Plus is unchanged.

Existing subscribers got the new allowance automatically, without waiting for renewal. One caveat from OpenAI: a larger allowance "does not guarantee twice as many completed tasks".

Does ChatGPT Pro have a 5-hour limit?

Not in Work and Codex. OpenAI says "Pro 100, Pro 200 and Pro 500 currently have no five-hour usage limit in Work and Codex." Your plan still has an included allowance, and Settings → Usage shows what's left and when it resets. Plus and standard Business seats do have a 5-hour window, and weekly limits may also apply.

What does Ultrafast on Pro 500 cost?

Ultrafast uses your included allowance at 8× the Standard rate for the same model. Once that's gone, it draws from your credits at 6× the Standard credit rate. It can drain even Pro 500's allowance quickly, so save it for work where speed matters.

Buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 doesn't unlock Ultrafast. Fast mode is available more widely and uses your allowance at 2.5×.

Upgrading for Ultrafast changes the maths. Pro 500 has the biggest allowance, but every Ultrafast turn costs as much as eight Standard turns. If you pay for Pro 500 mainly to run Ultrafast all day, expect it to run out much faster than the extra price suggests.

Is GPT-6 Pro in Chat part of the same allowance?

No. GPT-6 Pro in Chat has its own weekly message limits, separate from the Work and Codex allowance. Pro 100 and Pro 500 kept their weekly GPT-6 Pro limits. On Pro 200, the lower weekly limit now matches the higher one.

Will this change in December?

Yes, partly. Starting in December, Plus, Pro and Business plans' allowance will be shared across ChatGPT and Codex, measured in tokens. Instant chat won't count against it (Understanding ChatGPT and Codex usage limits). OpenAI hasn't said how the Pro tiers' allowances will change with it.

Which Pro tier should you pick?

Start from what actually runs out:

  • You run out on Plus a few times a month. Buying credits may be cheaper than jumping to Pro. Plus and Pro can buy credits without changing plans, and credits are used only after your included usage.
  • You run out on Plus every week. Pro 100 removes the 5-hour window and has a much bigger pool.
  • You run out on Pro 100 or Pro 200. Move up a tier in Settings → My Plan.
  • You want Ultrafast. Only Pro 500 has it.

FAQ

Can support raise or reset my Pro limits?

No. OpenAI says support can't reset ChatGPT or Codex limits. It can look into usage that was counted wrongly.

Does Pro 200 include Ultrafast now?

No. Ultrafast is only on Pro 500.

Is there a yearly Pro plan?

No. Pro is billed monthly.
