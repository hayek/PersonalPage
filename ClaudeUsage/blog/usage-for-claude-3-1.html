Usage for Claude 3.1

Multiple accounts, made solid — plus a sharper chart and faster Claude Code stats.

August 13, 2026

Accounts stay separate

Two accounts inside the same organization — or one login spanning several — now each keep their own usage, history, widgets, and Watch data. Nothing blends together anymore.

Sign-in that pairs correctly

Signing in now matches your session to the right account by identity instead of guessing, fixing accounts that got stuck, mismatched, or silently repointed after re-authenticating.

Choose your organization

If your Claude login belongs to more than one organization, the app asks which one to track instead of picking for you.

Name your accounts

Give each account a name that makes sense to you — “Work”, “Personal” — from the Accounts pane on Mac or the account hub on iPhone.

Notification rules follow you

Your rules now sync across Mac and iPhone through iCloud, with a per-rule “this device only” switch for the ones that should stay put.

Weekly line on the chart

History ranges from 12 hours to 7 days now overlay your weekly usage line, and weekly bars that hit the limit turn a darker, urgent orange.

Also included Claude Code stats parse incrementally and index every project folder, so they load faster and count everything; copy your usage snapshot as an image straight from the menu bar; unrecognized plans are no longer labelled “Free”, and Team plans get their own badge; the remaining-percentage mode now reads “42% left” everywhere, widgets included; plus fixes and translations across 16 languages. Thanks for using Usage for Claude!
