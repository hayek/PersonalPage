Your free full Claude reset expires December 10

Anthropic gave Pro, Max, Ultra and Team users one free reset of all their limits, across all models. If you haven't used it yet, you have until December 10 at 2:00 PM PT. Here's where to find it, and the catch.

November 26, 2026 · 3 min read

I built Usage for Claude so you can see how much limit you have left before you spend a reset →

What you got

On November 10, Anthropic's developer account announced a free full reset for paid subscribers, across all models and limits. It's a one-time reset you can save and use whenever you choose, up to the deadline.

We've given all Pro, Max, Ultra, and Team users one free full reset, across all models and limits. Use it anytime before December 10 at 2:00PM PT.

We recently fixed a few small issues that used more limits than intended, and the Fable resets didn't do much for those of you who'd…

— ClaudeDevs (@ClaudeDevs) November 10, 2026

Anthropic gave two reasons in the same post: it had fixed a few small issues that used more of people's limits than intended, and the Fable resets from the Fable 5.2 launch didn't help people who had already hit their weekly limits on every model.

Who has one

All Pro, Max, Ultra and Team users, according to @ClaudeDevs. The post doesn't mention the Free plan or Enterprise, and Anthropic hasn't said whether either got one. If you downgrade or cancel before using it, it's no longer available.

How to use it

  1. Open Settings → Usage in Claude on the web, in the desktop app or in the mobile app (source). The post also gives a shortcut: clau.de/reset.
  2. Find the Limit resets section and check the expiry shown on the offer. An unused reset expires on the day and time listed on the offer.
  3. Click “Reset for free” and confirm. The same button also appears on the message you see when you reach a limit.

The button isn't available in Claude Code in your terminal or IDE. Your limits are shared across your account, so a reset you apply in the browser, desktop or mobile app counts in Claude Code too (source).

The catch. It's one use, and once you use it you can't undo it. Your weekly limit still resets on its usual day and time, so the reset doesn't move your week. It doesn't refund extra usage you've already been billed for or change your usage credit balance (source). It's also separate from any Fable reset you got at the Fable 5.2 launch, which has its own expiry. Anthropic hasn't said more about how the two interact, so check what each offer in Settings → Usage says it restores.

When it's worth spending

You don't have to hit a limit before you use it (source). But a reset is worth the most when you've used up most of your weekly limit and still have work to do. If you still have plenty left, spending it now mostly throws it away. If you get close to your limit before December 10, that's the moment.

Usage for Claude shows your session and weekly limits side by side in the menu bar, on your iPhone and on your Apple Watch, so you can see how much you'd actually get back before you click. After you apply the reset, the bars drop back down on the next refresh.

Heads up Usage for Claude is an independent app, not affiliated with Anthropic. Offer details can change, so check Anthropic's post, the help article and your own Settings → Usage for the latest.
