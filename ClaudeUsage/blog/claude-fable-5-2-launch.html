Claude Fable 5.2 is here, with extra Fable usage this week

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 on November 9. For launch week, Pro and standard Team seats can use Fable inside their plan limits, and most paid plans get one to three free Fable resets. Here's who gets what, how to use a reset, and the catch.

November 10, 2026 · 3 min read

I built Usage for Claude to see these limits before you hit them →

What changed

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, the same underlying model. Fable 5.2 is the one you can use in Claude. Mythos 5.2 is only for organizations in Anthropic's verification programs.

  • Faster. Anthropic says Fable 5.2 writes its answers up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1.
  • Cheaper on the API. It costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, down from $10 and $50 for Fable 5.1, with a 1M-token context window (API release notes).
  • The new default Fable in Claude Code. Claude Code 2.1.321 made claude-fable-5-2 the default Fable model. In the Claude apps and Claude Code it runs at medium effort by default.

Who gets the launch offers

Normally, Fable isn't on the Free plan. Pro and standard Team seats pay for it with usage credits, and Max, Ultra and premium Team seats can spend up to half their weekly limit on it. For launch, Anthropic added two offers:

  • Pro and standard Team seats: from November 9 through November 16, 2026 at 11:59 pm PT, Fable is included in your plan for up to 50% of your weekly limits, so it doesn't use usage credits. You also get one Fable reset, which expires at the same time.
  • Max, Ultra, premium Team seats and premium seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans: one to three Fable resets, depending on how long you've paid. Less than six months gets one, six to 12 months gets two, more than 12 months gets three. On Team and Enterprise, the count follows the organization's first paid bill. These resets expire on March 8, 2027 at 11:59 pm PT.

Resets go only to accounts with paid billing in place before November 9, 2026 at 10:00 am PT. A subscription still in a free trial gets its resets once it converts to paid. The offers don't apply to the Free plan, standard seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans, self-serve Enterprise or usage-based Enterprise. HIPAA-ready Enterprise plans can't use Fable resets (source).

How to use a Fable reset

  1. Open Settings → Usage in Claude.
  2. Under Limit resets, select “Reset for free.” The usage comes back right away.

You don't have to wait until you hit a limit. You can use a reset any time before it expires. Fable 5.2 also doesn't become your default model: pick it in the model picker to use it (source).

The catch: a reset doesn't raise your limits. A Fable reset gives back the Fable usage on your weekly limit. Your usage on other models stays where it is, and the 50% cap on Fable doesn't change. If your plan has a 5-hour limit, a reset also refills that whole window. Once used, a reset can't be undone. If you cancel or downgrade before using it, you lose it. Fable also uses your limits faster than other models. After the launch week, or once a Pro or standard Team seat has used its 50%, Fable goes back to usage credits, and only if you have them turned on (source). Anthropic hasn't said whether Fable 5.1 stays in the model picker.

Keep an eye on the Fable bar

Because Fable draws down your limits faster, launch week is an easy time to run out early. When Claude reports a separate weekly Fable limit for your account, Usage for Claude shows it as its own Fable bar next to your session and weekly limits, in the menu bar, on your iPhone and on your Apple Watch. A notification rule on that bar tells you when to save your reset, or when to switch back to another model.
