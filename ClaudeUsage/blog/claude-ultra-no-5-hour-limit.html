Max 20x is now Ultra, with no 5-hour limit

Anthropic renamed its two Max plans on October 30. The top one, now called Ultra, also drops the 5-hour session limit for verified accounts. Your weekly limit doesn't change. Here's who gets it, how to check, and what it doesn't do.

November 1, 2026 · 3 min read

I built Usage for Claude to see these limits before you hit them →

What changed

Two things, both announced by Anthropic on October 30:

  • New names, same prices. Max 5x is now just Max ($100 a month) and Max 20x is now Ultra ($200 a month). If you already subscribe, you don't need to do anything (source).
  • No 5-hour limit on verified Ultra accounts. Normally, paid plans have a session limit that resets every 5 hours, plus a weekly limit on top. Verified Ultra accounts no longer have the 5-hour one (source).

We’re renaming our Max plans:

Max 5x → Max
Max 20x → Ultra

Max is still $100/month, and Ultra is still $200. If you’re a subscriber, there’s nothing you need to do.

Who gets it

Only Ultra (the plan formerly called Max 20x). Anthropic says existing Max 20x subscribers move to Ultra automatically, with no price change, and that the 5-hour limit is coming off starting with most existing subscribers. Anthropic hasn't said when the rest will get it.

Max (formerly Max 5x) keeps its 5-hour limit: it still gives five times the Pro plan's usage every five hours and every week. Pro, Team and Enterprise aren't part of this change.

How to check if your account is verified

Verification is automatic. Anthropic checks factors like your account and payment history to prevent fraud and abuse, and lifts the limit when it's done. Until then, a temporary 5-hour limit applies.

  1. Open Settings → Usage on claude.ai.
  2. Look for a “Current session” bar. If it's not there, your account is verified and has no 5-hour limit.

Verifying your phone number at checkout is a separate step and doesn't remove the limit. Anthropic hasn't said how long automatic verification takes (source).

The catch: the weekly limit is still there. Ultra still has a weekly limit across all models, which the pricing page puts at 10× Pro usage per week, plus a separate weekly limit for Fable. Without a session limit, you can use that weekly allowance faster, not get more of it. Anthropic also says additional usage limits may apply to manage capacity and fair access. And the $200 price is for web subscriptions. Mobile pricing may differ, and Ultra is monthly only for now.

What to watch now

Once the session limit is gone, the weekly limit is the one that stops you. A long Claude Code run used to pause at the 5-hour mark. Now it can keep going until it reaches your weekly cap, and the weekly limit takes days to come back, not hours.
