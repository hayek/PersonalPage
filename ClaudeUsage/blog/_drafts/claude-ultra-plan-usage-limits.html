Claude Ultra plan usage limits: no 5-hour limit, explained

Max 20x is now called Ultra. It costs the same $200 a month, gives you 10 times Pro's usage every week, and once your account is verified it has no 5-hour limit at all. The weekly limit is the only one left to watch.

{{DATE_LONG}} · 4 min read

What is the Claude Ultra plan?

Ultra is Anthropic's top individual plan. It replaces Max 20x: "The Max 20x plan is now called the Ultra plan," and existing Max 20x subscribers moved to Ultra automatically, with no price change (What is the Ultra plan?).

It's $200 a month on the web, billed monthly only. Prices in the iPhone and Android apps may differ. Like Max, it includes Claude Code, priority access to new models and monthly credits for the Claude API.

Does Claude Ultra have a 5-hour limit?

Not if your account is verified. Anthropic's wording: verified Ultra accounts "don't have a five-hour limit". You can use your usage as fast or as slowly as you like, as long as you stay inside the weekly limit.

If your account isn't verified yet, a temporary limit applies. Anthropic verifies Ultra accounts automatically, "using factors like your account and payment history", and removes the temporary limit by itself. There's nothing to apply for. Verifying your phone number at checkout is a separate step and doesn't remove it.

How do I check if my Ultra account is verified?

Open Settings › Usage on claude.ai. If there's no Current session bar, your account is verified. If the bar is still there, the temporary limit is still on.

How much usage does Ultra get compared to Max and Pro?

  • Max ($100/month): 5 times Pro's usage every five hours and every week. Max keeps both the 5-hour limit and the weekly limit (What is the Max plan?).
  • Ultra ($200/month): 10 times Pro's usage every week, and no 5-hour limit once verified.

Note the change in wording. Max 20x used to be described per session ("20x Pro"). Ultra is described per week ("10x Pro every week"). So Ultra's promise is about how much you get in a week, plus the freedom to spend it in big bursts. Anthropic doesn't publish absolute numbers for either plan.

Does Ultra still have a weekly limit?

Yes, two of them, the same as Max:

  • A weekly limit across all models. On Max it resets at a fixed time each week assigned to your account, and Settings › Usage shows the next reset.
  • A separate weekly limit for Fable. You can use up to 50% of your weekly limit on Fable models. That comes out of the same weekly pool, not on top of it, and Fable uses it faster than other models (Fable models on your plan).

Anthropic also says it may add other limits to manage capacity, such as monthly caps or limits on specific models and features.

No session limit means no safety brake. On Max, the 5-hour limit stops a runaway day before it eats the week. On a verified Ultra account nothing stops you until the weekly bar is full, so a few heavy days of parallel Claude Code agents can use up the whole week. Check your weekly percentage against how much of the week is left.

Does Claude Code share Ultra's limits with claude.ai?

Yes. The weekly limit and the Fable limit are shared across Claude and Claude Code, so everything you do in the Claude apps and in Claude Code counts against the same pool (Claude Code with your Pro, Max, or Ultra plan).

One small change for Claude Code users: the "Usage limit reached · wrapping up" allowance, which lets Claude Code finish a step when you hit the 5-hour limit, doesn't apply to verified Ultra accounts, because there's no 5-hour limit to hit. While the temporary limit is on, it works like it does on Max.

What happens when an Ultra account hits the weekly limit?

You have three options, per Anthropic:

  • Turn on usage credits to keep going after the weekly limit or the Fable limit, billed separately from your subscription.
  • Switch to a Claude Console account and pay API rates for a heavy sprint.
  • Wait for the weekly reset.

If you have a limit reset saved, on Ultra it refills your weekly limits. The monthly API credits that come with Ultra can't be used in Claude Code or the Claude apps.

Should you pick Ultra or Max?

Look at which limit stops you today. If you keep hitting the 5-hour limit on Max but finish the week with room to spare, Ultra's main gain is removing that wall. If you hit Max's weekly limit days before the reset, Ultra's doubled weekly usage is what you're paying for. If neither happens, Max is probably enough. Upgrading from Max to Ultra is charged pro rata for the rest of your billing cycle.

FAQ

Is Claude Ultra the same as Max 20x?

Yes. Max 20x was renamed Ultra, and subscribers moved over automatically at the same price.

Why do I still see a 5-hour limit on Ultra?

Your account isn't verified yet. The temporary limit goes away automatically once it is.

Does Max still have a 5-hour limit?

Yes. Max and Pro keep both the 5-hour limit and the weekly limit.

Is Ultra available as a yearly plan?

Not right now. Ultra is monthly only.
