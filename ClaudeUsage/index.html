Questions

What is Usage for Claude + ChatGPT?
A Mac menu bar app, with apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, that tracks your Claude and ChatGPT usage limits. It reads your session and weekly limits straight from each account and refreshes in the background, so you can see where you stand without opening a browser. It tracks several accounts at once, Claude and ChatGPT alike, and everything syncs through your own iCloud.
What's new in version 4?
Three view styles for your account stack (Minimal, Detailed and Chart), ChatGPT accounts with their Codex limits beside Claude, a redesigned Settings window with six tabs, several accounts side by side in the Mac menu bar, per-account widgets and alert rules, automatic switching to the account you're using, and the full app on iPad. See everything new in 4.0.
Does it track ChatGPT and Codex usage?
Yes, exactly like Claude. Add a ChatGPT account and the app shows your Codex session and weekly limits in the popover, the Dashboard, widgets, the menu bar, history and alert rules. You sign in on the official ChatGPT page inside the app, and the app watches ChatGPT's service status the same way it watches Claude's.
How do I check my Claude usage limit?
Claude shows your current limits at claude.ai/settings/usage. Usage for Claude reads the same numbers and keeps them in your Mac menu bar, on your iPhone and in widgets, refreshing in the background, so you can check your session and weekly limits at a glance without opening a browser.
Is this an official Anthropic or OpenAI app?
No. It's an independent, third-party app built by a Claude enthusiast for people who use Claude and Codex. It is not created, endorsed or maintained by Anthropic or OpenAI.
Which accounts are supported?
Claude Pro, Max and Team accounts, and ChatGPT Plus, Pro and Business accounts. Claude accounts show the session and weekly limits from claude.ai/settings/usage; ChatGPT accounts show their Codex session and weekly limits. Free plans don't report usage, and Enterprise accounts aren't supported.
Can I track multiple accounts?
Yes. Add your personal, work and ChatGPT accounts and they appear as one stack of cards, in the view style you choose. Tap or click a card to switch, or let the app switch to the account you're actually using. Each account keeps its own usage, history and plan badge, and every account refreshes in the background. On the Mac, each account can also have its own menu bar segment. One account is free; keeping several at once is a Pro feature.
What are the view styles?
Minimal shows the active account in full and the other accounts as one line each. Detailed gives every account its own bars. Chart draws the current session as it happens for the active account, with a small chart for each of the others. Pick the style in Settings; it never changes which account is active.
How do I sign in?
Click the menu bar icon and choose Add Account. A window opens where you sign in through Claude's or ChatGPT's official website, and you can do the same directly on your iPhone or iPad. Because Google blocks sign-in inside embedded windows, Google account holders should use the email code option.
Is there an iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch app?
Yes. The iPhone and iPad app is fully featured: sign in on the device itself and get the same account stack, session chart, history, insights, activity grid and alerts, plus Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Version 4 lays the app out for the iPad's screen. The Apple Watch app shows your limits as complications. Everything syncs across your devices through your own iCloud account.
Do I need the Mac app for the iPhone app to work?
No. Sign in directly on your iPhone and it fetches your usage on its own. If you use both, they stay in sync through iCloud, and each device can keep its own active account.
When do Claude's 5-hour and weekly limits reset?
The session limit runs on a 5-hour window, and the weekly limit resets once a week at a time set for your account. Anthropic also resets limits early from time to time. Codex limits on ChatGPT work the same way, with their own 5-hour and weekly windows. The app shows when each limit resets, marks every reset on the History chart, and can notify you the moment a limit resets.
Will I get notified before I hit a limit?
Yes. Alert rules fire when usage crosses a threshold, when a limit resets, when you're on pace to hit a limit, or a set time before a reset. Each rule can watch one account, all of them, or whichever is active. Instead of building a rule by hand you can describe it, like “warn me at 80%”, and the app turns your words into rules you can review before saving. That runs on Apple Intelligence, so your text is parsed on device and never leaves it.
Does the app tell me when Claude or ChatGPT is down?
Yes. The app monitors both services' status pages and shows a colored dot on the menu bar icon with a banner explaining the incident when there's an outage or degraded performance. You can also turn on a service status alert rule to be notified, and notified again when things are back to normal.
Does it work with Claude Code and Codex CLI?
Yes. Claude Code counts toward the same 5-hour and weekly limits as Claude itself, and Codex CLI counts toward your ChatGPT account's Codex limits, so the menu bar already reflects both. The Dashboard can also show your Claude Code stats: messages, tool calls, sessions and token usage by model, read from the local stats file on your Mac, which never leaves your device.
Do I need to update the app when a new model appears?
No. The app reads its usage schema remotely, so when Anthropic adds a new model or usage window it appears in your popover and Dashboard automatically, with no App Store update needed.
Does the app store my password?
No. Sign-in happens entirely on the official Claude or ChatGPT website inside the app, and the app only keeps the session needed to stay logged in. Your password is never stored.
Where is my data stored, and is it shared?
Your usage stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account, and nothing is sold or shared. The app talks to claude.ai and chatgpt.com directly to fetch your usage, with no server of ours in between, plus the two services' status pages. Anonymous analytics and crash reports help fix bugs, and the privacy policy lists everything.
Is the app free? How much does it cost?
The app is free to download and use. The menu bar, widgets, Apple Watch, alerts and service status are free, and one account is always free. Pro adds the full History dashboard with charts, insights and the activity grid, sharing, Claude Code stats, the Local API and tracking several accounts at once, as a monthly or annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, with a free trial. One purchase unlocks Mac, iPhone and iPad.
What are the system requirements?
The Mac app needs macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later, on both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. The iPhone and iPad app needs iOS or iPadOS 26.2 or later, and the Apple Watch app needs watchOS 26.4 or later. An internet connection is required to fetch your usage.
