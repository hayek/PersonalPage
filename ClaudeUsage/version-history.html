Version History

See what's new in Usage for Claude

v3.0

Big update: manage every Claude account from your menu bar.

Multiple Accounts

Add your work and personal Claude accounts and switch between them from the menu bar title. No signing out, no re-logging in. Each account keeps its own usage, history, and plan badge, and all accounts refresh quietly in the background so the one you switch to is already up to date. The Accounts pane in Settings has been rebuilt around multi-account support. Your first account is free; tracking more at once is a premium feature.

Your Usage on iPhone, Too

The companion iPhone app is now fully featured. Sign in directly on your iPhone without needing your Mac, view the same dashboard, session history, insights, activity grid, and usage windows, and set alerts by describing them — for example, "warn me at 80%". Your Mac and iPhone stay in sync through iCloud.

Service Status Alerts

Know when the issue is on Claude's side. The app monitors Anthropic's service status and shows a banner with the details during an outage or degraded performance, so you can stop troubleshooting your own setup. Turn on a status rule to be notified as well.

New Limits Appear Automatically

When Anthropic adds a new model or usage window, it appears in your dashboard and menu bar automatically. No app update needed.

Faster, Lighter, Steadier

We rebuilt performance from the ground up to reduce background disk and CPU work dramatically. That means better battery life, and less app activity if you previously noticed the app writing to disk for long periods. Charts load faster, and we fixed a launch crash plus a set of sign-in issues that could leave usage stuck at 0% or 100%.

Also included: a refreshed account switcher and paywall, live progress-bar previews in Settings, and dozens of polish fixes across 16 languages. Found a bug, or something you'd use every day? Click the speech-bubble icon in the menu bar to send feedback in seconds — every message is read. Thanks for using Usage for Claude!

v2.6

Big update: Claude usage is now easier to see and act on across every device.

Apple Watch Companion

A new watchOS app brings your Claude usage to your wrist. It syncs via iCloud, includes complications for every watch face, and shows your usage at a glance.

Threshold Notifications

Stay in control with alerts that trigger when your session, weekly, or Opus usage hits your limits. You can also get notified when those periods reset. All notification controls are now in the new Notifications settings tab.

Widgets Everywhere

Check usage without opening the app. Get new iOS lock screen widgets and Apple Watch complications, alongside the existing Mac desktop widgets and iOS home screen widgets.

Local API for Claude Code

Connect your live usage to AI agents and dashboards using a local HTTP endpoint. A bundled skill markdown is included, so you can get from setup to integration faster.

Redesigned Dashboard

Make decisions with clearer views: a Current Session chart for real-time progress, a Session History bar chart with filters, refreshed Insights cards, plus a resizable window and adjustable font size for better readability.

New Settings Experience

A native preferences window organizes everything into General, Appearance, Notifications, and Sync panes. You can also hide your account email and show remaining percentage in the menu bar.

We also refreshed the Help Center (now available in 15 languages), redesigned the macOS feedback form, and shipped dozens of polish improvements. Thanks for using Usage for Claude!
