# What Did I Miss landing page

- **Search basics:** title, meta description, canonical, Open Graph/Twitter (absolute `Assets/og-image.jpg`, 1200×630), `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD and `FAQPage` JSON-LD (`id="faqData"`, rebuilt by `i18n/build.py` from the FAQ cards; never hand-edit it). Feature lead-ins are `<h2 class="feature-lead">`, styled as inline bold. The site-wide sitemap/robots live at the PersonalPage root (hand-maintained; add the language URLs there too).
- **Translated pages:** `i18n/build.py` (adapted from `MyBusStop/i18n/build.py`) cuts `index.html` into text units (`i18n/en.json`) and builds `<lang>/index.html` for each `i18n/<lang>.json`: the app's 30 languages (WhatDidIMiss/Localizable.xcstrings), with `hreflang`, canonical, `og:locale`, `lang`/`dir`, `../` paths, FAQ JSON-LD and footer language links. After changing the English page: `python3 i18n/build.py extract` (new/changed sentences land in `i18n/todo/<lang>.json`), translate those keys into `i18n/<lang>.json` using the app's string catalogs as the glossary, `python3 i18n/build.py check <lang>`, then `python3 i18n/build.py build`. Never edit `<lang>/index.html` by hand.
- **Version:** `scripts/release.sh` in the app repo bumps `class="download-version">Version x.y.z` in the English page and runs `build`; the units hold it as `{{VERSION}}`, and `build` also writes `softwareVersion`.
- Arabic and Hebrew are RTL: the menu screenshot column moves to the left; the screenshots stay LTR (end of styles.css).
