Frequently Asked Questions

What is Vanishing List?

Vanishing List is a checklist app where lists vanish on their own. It's great for shopping lists, packing, errands and anything else you only need for a while. Check items off as you go, and the list cleans itself up afterwards.

How do lists vanish?

Each list has a timer based on when you last changed it. When the timer runs out, the list moves to Trash. You can choose 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days under “Vanish after” in Preferences.

What resets a list's timer?

Any change to a list resets its timer: editing the title, adding, editing, deleting or reordering items, and checking or unchecking them. A list you're still using stays around, and one you've stopped touching vanishes.

How do I import a list someone sent me?

Touch and hold the message, tap Share, then choose Vanishing List. Or copy the text and tap Paste List in the app. On Mac, drag the text onto the window or press ⌘⇧V. Messy text opens a preview first so you can check the items before adding them.

What is Smart Import?

Smart Import uses Apple Intelligence, on your device, to tidy up messy text. It picks the items out of a chatty message, splits “milk, eggs and two avocados” into separate items, and pulls out quantities like “2 L” or “a dozen”. Without Apple Intelligence, the built-in cleanup takes over automatically.

Is Vanishing List free?

Vanishing List starts with a 7-day free trial with every feature. After the trial, you can keep 1 list for free, and a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited lists. There is no subscription.

Do my lists sync across devices?

Yes. Your lists sync through iCloud between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on each device.

Can I use Siri and Shortcuts?

Yes. Say “Add a list in Vanishing List” or “What's on my Vanishing List”. In the Shortcuts app you'll find Add List, Get Lists and Open List, and you can set the Action Button to Add List.

Can I restore a deleted list?

Yes. Lists that vanish or that you delete move to Trash for 30 days. Open Preferences, scroll to Trash and tap Restore. A restored list gets a fresh timer.

Where is my data stored?

Your lists are stored on your device and in your private iCloud account. Smart Import runs entirely on your device. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports help improve the app, but the contents of your lists, their titles and your clipboard are never sent.
