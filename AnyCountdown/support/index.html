Common questions

My widget is not updating

Widgets are refreshed by the system rather than on a fixed clock, so a countdown can lag by a few minutes. Opening the app forces a refresh. If a widget stays blank, remove it from the Home Screen and add it again — that rebuilds its timeline from scratch.

I bought countdowns and they have not appeared

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that made them, so make sure you are signed in with the same one. If the balance is still wrong, email us with the date of the purchase and we will sort it out.

How do I cancel Any Countdown Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling leaves every countdown you have already made exactly where it is.

Do I have to use the AI?

No. Building a countdown by hand, with a date you set yourself, is free and unlimited. The AI is only involved when you describe something and ask the app to design it.

I want a countdown taken out of the Public Gallery

If it is yours, turn its share toggle off — the public copy and its picture are deleted. If it is someone else's, use the ⋯ menu on the card to report or block it. Reported content is hidden automatically, and anything objectionable is removed within 24 hours. You can also email us.

What happens to my data?

The short version: your countdowns stay on your device and in your own private iCloud, and nothing becomes public unless you deliberately share it. The long version is in the privacy policy, which also explains how to ask for a copy of what we hold or have it erased.

Reporting something urgent

If you have found a security problem, or content in the gallery that needs to come down immediately, put URGENT in the subject line and it will be looked at first.
